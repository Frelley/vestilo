# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young adults (18–35) in Santa Cruz, Bolivia browsing for unique, affordable t-shirts. Mobile-first shoppers who discover through swiping or scrolling, save favorites, and complete orders via WhatsApp. The experience is casual and social — they're browsing on the go, not doing serious shopping research.

## Product Purpose

Vestilo is a boutique clothing catalogue for a local Bolivian t-shirt seller. The site replaces a WhatsApp catalogue by giving buyers a delightful browsing experience (swipe or grid), AI-powered search, and a direct path to WhatsApp checkout. Success means browsers becoming buyers through a fun, effortless discovery flow.

## Brand Personality

Local, playful, warm. The brand voice is informal and personal ("a tu sonso" — deliberately casual Bolivian slang). Not a big brand; a person selling clothes to their community.

## Anti-references

- Generic fast-fashion mass-market sites (Shein, AliExpress) — impersonal and overwhelming
- Sterile e-commerce templates — cold, corporate, no personality
- Over-produced luxury brand aesthetics — mismatched with the local/informal tone

## Design Principles

1. **Discovery over search** — browsing should feel like flipping through a friend's wardrobe, not querying a database
2. **Local over global** — the design should feel rooted in Santa Cruz, not globally optimized
3. **Warmth over polish** — a slightly imperfect, handmade quality beats corporate shine
4. **Mobile first, always** — every interaction is designed for one thumb on a phone screen
5. **WhatsApp is the CTA** — every flow leads to a natural WhatsApp tap, not a checkout form

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG requirements stated. Spanish-language interface throughout. Touch targets should be generous for mobile use. Color filter uses soft-sort (never hides items) to accommodate color perception differences.
